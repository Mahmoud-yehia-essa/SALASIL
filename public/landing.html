The Process

How SALASIL Works

From first request to final delivery — a seamlessly orchestrated journey in four simple steps.

01

Place Your Request

Describe your shipment details — cargo type, pickup location, destination, and preferred timeline. Our AI-powered system instantly matches you with the ideal truck and driver from our vetted network.

02

Driver Assigned Instantly

A verified, professionally-rated driver accepts your booking. You receive their profile, vehicle details, and direct contact — full transparency before the journey even starts.

03

Track Every Kilometer

Monitor your shipment live on an interactive map. Receive proactive ETA updates, milestone notifications, and condition reports — total peace of mind throughout transit.

04

Delivered & Confirmed

Digital proof of delivery with e-signature and timestamped photo documentation. Automated invoice generation and instant payment processing — the entire cycle, closed seamlessly.
